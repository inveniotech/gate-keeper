# E-NNN: Decision title

## Context

Describe the engineering problem and constraints using sources suitable for this
repository. Keep confidential information out of public records.

## Decision

State the proposed approach and link to the specification that owns its details.
Do not duplicate a format contract here.

## Status

**Draft — awaiting owner review and approval.** Only the owner may approve a decision.
When the owner authorises recognising implemented or previously accepted decisions,
record that authority, the date and links to the implementation or prior acceptance.
For partial acceptance, list the accepted decisions and the specific details still
awaiting review separately. Do not treat pending implementation as pending approval
when an owner decision is already recorded.

## Consequences

Explain the costs, benefits and obligations that follow from the approach.

## Alternatives considered

Explain the relevant alternatives and their trade-offs. Distinguish proposed
rationale from alternatives the owner has already considered.

## Traces

List the work package, public issue, applicable requirement identifiers and source
links. A record of an existing engineering-plan decision cites its R-number.
